# Dashboard purpose and information architecture

## The five questions the landing page must answer
1. Do we have enough evidence?
2. Is anything blocking the selected-CSO pilot?
3. Where is the learner experience fragile?
4. What must DEC act on before the next stage?
5. What can safely wait for selected-CSO validation, wider release or later programme phases?

## Recommended pages

### 1. Pilot Overview
Separate compact readiness panels for Learning Hub, HRBA and Project Management; evidence coverage; Final Reviews received; unresolved critical issues; critical checks lacking evidence; open High actions due now/before selected-CSO pilot; Decision Horizon distribution; priority findings/actions; last sync.

### 2. Pilot Coverage & Progress
Tester/course/device coverage, completion state, special verification, Final Review receipt, Quick Finding activity and unresolved possible blockers. Do not treat this as course-quality scoring.

### 3. Practical Checks
All required practical learner-journey checks with PASS / PASS WITH ISSUE / FAIL / NOT TESTED distributions, evidence count and drill-through. Highlight critical checks without creating a composite score.

### 4. Quality Review
Six domains: Access & Entry; Learning Experience; Learning & Better Decisions; Context & Relevance; Application & Transfer; DEC Operational Readiness. Show rating distributions and indicator-level evidence.

### 5. Learning & Better Decisions
Common decision/application indicators plus separate HRBA and Project Management course-specific indicators. Use the Capacity-to-Action annexes for interpretation.

### 6. Context, Application & Transfer
Context/relevance, workplace-use examples, adaptable tools, support needs, device/internet patterns and transfer evidence.

### 7. Findings & Blockers
Quick Finding stream, categories, screenshots where available, possible blockers, repeated patterns and source-record drill-through.

### 8. Finding-to-Action
Operational workspace with Finding ID, Course/Hub, Domain, Evidence, Source Record IDs, evidence count, recurrence, Severity, Blocker Classification, Interpretation, Decision Category, Recommended Action, Priority, Responsible Person/Unit, Target Timing, Status, Verification/Result, Response Area, Finding Type and Decision Horizon.

### 9. Qualitative Evidence
Search/filter exact tester comments and practical examples. Dedicated views for KEEP, Priority Improvement, Possible Blocker, confusing/difficult activity, useful tool/practice, support needs and recommendations. Preserve raw wording and record traceability.

### 10. Readiness & Decision Record
Separate Learning Hub / HRBA / PM evidence signals, unresolved vs verified-closed critical actions, critical evidence gaps, High actions due before selected-CSO pilot, and a separate human DEC final readiness decision record.

## Interaction model
- Global filters: course/system, date, device, internet experience, practical result, quality rating, finding category, blocker status, Decision Horizon; tester filter only where operationally appropriate.
- Drill-through from every decision metric to supporting records.
- Searchable evidence drawer/side panel is preferable to opening raw database tables.
- Keep the executive page concise; details belong in drill-down pages.
